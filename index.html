Is coaching actually free?

How do I buy a tweak pack?

Click Buy Pack and you'll be taken to the Discord server to arrange the purchase.

Will tweaks guarantee higher FPS?

No. Performance varies by PC hardware, game settings, drivers and other factors. Tweaks are not a guarantee of a specific FPS result.
